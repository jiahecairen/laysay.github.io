What a New Phone Taught Me新手机礼物给我的警示

2022-03-15 | Thinks随想

When I got the iPhone 13, I felt fairly calm. I thought it was a gift I did not really need. What mattered more was what it made me realize.

The way we show care can be completely different from the way someone else hopes to receive it.

I think she does not care when she spends the day on her phone, hardly talks to me and only asks for a shoulder or leg massage. She thinks I do not care because I spend all day making good food but rush her through the shops as if we are in a funeral procession.

In the end, we are speaking different languages.

Chinese version

新手机礼物给我的警示

其实收到iPhone13的时候，我的内心是平静的，只是觉得没必要……
真正有价值的是它带给我的警示：
每个人对别人好方式，以及期待别人对自己好的方式，是完全隔绝互不相通的。
我觉得她天天玩手机不搭理我，只想着我给她捏肩捶腿，这是不在乎我的表现。
她觉得我天天只知道做好吃的，逛街的时候就像在赶尸，这是不在乎她的表现。
其实，语言不通罢了。
